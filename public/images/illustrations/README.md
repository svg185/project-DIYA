# Storybook illustrations

The ten SVGs in this directory are original, lightweight storybook artwork
created for this project. They use only gradients and vector shapes, so they
load locally without third-party image URLs or licensing concerns. Each
chapter image is 16:9 and is referenced from `index.html` with descriptive
alternative text and lazy loading after the opening chapter.

## Art direction prompts

These prompts document the intended visual direction if higher-resolution
painted renders are added later:

1. **Envelope:** dreamy hand-painted anime storybook illustration of a mysterious glowing envelope floating in a purple and pink galaxy, golden stars, soft clouds, heart wax seal, romantic pastel watercolor atmosphere, no text, 16:9.
2. **Workspace:** fictional young Indian male student and technology enthusiast at a cozy wooden desk, abstract code on laptop, books, warm lamp, sunset window, watercolor storybook aesthetic, no readable text, 16:9.
3. **Storm:** empty property office during a rainy evening, city buildings through glass, papers on a wooden desk, blue-grey sky, warm interior light, emotional watercolor storybook aesthetic, 16:9.
4. **Fighter:** fictional young Indian man working late at a laptop with notebooks, warm lamp, rain outside, determined atmosphere, magical golden light, watercolor storybook aesthetic, 16:9.
5. **Midnight:** lonely figure beside a laptop late at night, moonlight through a window, peaceful city lights, dark blue and purple watercolor atmosphere, 16:9.
6. **Garden:** symbolic pastel rose garden with butterflies, cherry blossoms, golden sunlight, floating petals, dreamy magical watercolor atmosphere, no person, 16:9.
7. **Hints:** smartphone on a wooden desk near a window, glowing notification light, pink flowers, sunset, tiny heart particles, no readable screen text, watercolor storybook style, 16:9.
8. **New beginning:** dark lonely room transforming into a flower-filled garden, golden light through a window, butterflies and glowing flowers, emotional visual storytelling, 16:9.
9. **Confession:** glowing pink heart surrounded by rose petals and golden stars on a dreamy purple background, emotional cinematic watercolor atmosphere, 16:9.
10. **Proposal:** pink rose garden at sunset, heart-shaped floral arch, golden fairy lights, cherry blossom petals, glowing pastel sky, romantic watercolor storybook composition, 16:9.
